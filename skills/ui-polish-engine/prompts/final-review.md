## Final Questions

Ask:

1. Does the UI feel intentional?
2. Is every alignment relationship consistent?
3. Are the radius values systematic?
4. Are surfaces visually separated?
5. Is glass used intentionally?
6. Does motion communicate hierarchy?
7. Does anything animate unnecessarily?
8. Does the UI feel calm?
9. Does the UI feel premium?
